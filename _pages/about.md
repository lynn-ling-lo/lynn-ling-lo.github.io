### About Me
I received my Ph.D. from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Taiwan, where I worked in [AIMM Lab](https://aimm.cmlab.csie.ntu.edu.tw/index.html) under the mentorship of [Prof. Wen-Huang Cheng](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/zh_tw/member/Faculty/%E9%84%AD%E6%96%87%E7%9A%87-Wen-Huang-Cheng-80704716) and [Prof. Hong-Han Shuai](https://basiclab.lab.nycu.edu.tw/). Prior to that, I obtained my B.S. in Electronics from National Chiao Tung University, Taiwan. During my Ph.D., I was fortunate to spend time as a visiting student at UC Merced as a visiting student, where I collaborated with [Prof. Ming-Hsuan Yang](https://faculty.ucmerced.edu/mhyang/) and [Dr. Kelvin C.K. Chan](https://ckkelvinchan.github.io/) from Google DeepMind.

My research focuses on developing controllable and trustworthy visual AI systems that can understand, generate, and interact with the world. I am particularly interested in generative visual models, multimodal learning and understanding, and AI safety, with current research spanning image and video generation, multimodal visual alignment, image immunization, and content authentication.
